After trialing a few platforms, Sean landed on Roofr. “Some CRMs have more features, but they’re massive and not built for small businesses. Roofr was a great fit. It’s intuitive, scalable, and constantly improving.” “If I had to guess, it’s probably cut the time to place an order in half. But the game changer came when Roofr launched its QXO integration for material ordering. Sean joined the beta and immediately saw the impact.
