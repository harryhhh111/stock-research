How Sean O’Hare Streamlined Operations and Scaled Faster with Roofr + QXO

Sean O'Hare, Founder of Evolution RoofWorks

Talk to sales

The Challenge

When Sean O’Hare founded Evolution RoofWorks, he was determined to choose the right CRM from day one. As a serial entrepreneur, he knew the chaos of relying on spreadsheets and emails for operations. So when he launched his roofing business, Sean prioritized finding a platform that would evolve with him—without overwhelming his workflow.

After trialing a few platforms, Sean landed on Roofr.

“Some CRMs have more features, but they’re massive and not built for small businesses. Roofr was a great fit. It’s intuitive, scalable, and constantly improving.”
“If I had to guess, it’s probably cut the time to place an order in half. I used to spend so much time double-checking prices and sending things over manually to my supply rep.”

The Solution

From the beginning, Roofr became the backbone of Sean’s operations—handling measurements, quotes, and job management in one place. But the game changer came when Roofr launched its QXO integration for material ordering.

Sean joined the beta and immediately saw the impact.

Previously, he relied on manual processes—outdated pricing spreadsheets, back-and-forth emails with suppliers, and the constant fear of quoting the wrong price. Now, with real-time pricing embedded directly in the Roofr platform, Sean’s proposals are more accurate, and his margins are protected.

“These days, pricing is adjusting day by day—it’s super pertinent to have accurate numbers baked right into the quoting tool.”

And the integration goes beyond quoting. With just a few clicks, Sean’s material orders sync directly into QXO—reducing errors, saving time, and giving him peace of mind.

“In the early days, I’d prep all my proposals at home because one wrong price could mess with my margins. Now I show up to appointments with everything ready. I’ve got the measurements, the quote, and I know exactly what materials I need. It helps me sell more—and look more professional.”

Ready to see what Roofr can do for you?

The results

Roofr’s all-in-one approach means Sean no longer needs separate platforms for measurements, estimates, and material takeoffs. He can generate reports, build quotes, and manage jobs in a single place.

“Most CRMs still make you use outside tools. With Roofr, everything’s connected—including ordering. And with QXO, I know my pricing is right, and I’m not wasting hours sending orders or chasing my supplier. It’s just so much easier.”
