SiFive Paves the Path to Accelerated Adoption of RISC-V in the Datacenter with the BigSky Development Server

Powered by SiFive’s latest RISC-V CPU technology, the new development platform enables hands-on software porting, workload tuning, and validation testing for hyperscaler customers and ecosystem partners

SANTA CLARA, Calif. – August 24, 2026 – SiFive today, at an event coinciding with Hot Chips, will introduce the SiFive BigSky SF-2U870 datacenter development platform. In collaboration with hyperscaler customers and leading ecosystem partners, SiFive has delivered the world’s first enterprise-grade, rackable 2U RISC-V server. The platform will be demonstrated running a variety of server workloads at the event, and is built specifically for customers to accelerate RISC-V datacenter software porting, workload optimization, and system validation. This enables customers, partners and SiFive to accelerate the deployment of production-grade RISC-V solutions for the datacenter.

Lead customers are currently running workloads on deployed BigSky systems, including hyperscaler, software, and major SoC hardware leaders.

“SiFive is closely collaborating with industry leaders to accelerate deployment of RISC-V in the datacenter. With BigSky, we have delivered a powerful turnkey server platform designed to accelerate development of customer datacenter workloads and software on the RISC-V open standard,” said Patrick Little, Chairman and CEO, SiFive. “Ultimately, we are focused on enabling customers to meaningfully optimize the TCO of their custom SoC solutions with SiFive IP. RISC-V in the datacenter isn't a distant aspiration any more, it is happening right now, and SiFive is leading the way.”

"SiFive’s new development servers mark a major turning point in the industry. The amount of datacenter software they already have up and running clearly demonstrates just how far industry support for RISC-V has come in a remarkably short period of time." said Patrick Kennedy, President, Axautik Group/ServeTheHome.

Accelerating the Open Ecosystem with Broad Industry Support

Fully compliant with the RVA23 standard, the BigSky SF-2U870 platform eliminates the need for complex custom porting, enabling seamless, out-of-the-box deployment of enterprise software environments.

The platform features out-of-the-box support for standard enterprise-grade Linux releases, such as Canonical Ubuntu 26.04 LTS and Red Hat RHEL10.

SiFive is working with NVIDIA to port CUDA to SiFive based RISC-V hardware and integrate NVLink Fusion into future SiFive platforms. Demonstrating this progress, SiFive now has CUDA running on the P870-D-powered BigSky datacenter development platform. By serving as the head node for modern LLM workloads on NVIDIA GPUs, this platform delivers a capability never before seen on RISC-V hardware.

To support this milestone for the RISC-V architecture, industry leaders Canonical and Red Hat offered the following statements:

“At Canonical, we are committed to driving open-source innovation from developer environments to the datacenter core." said Gordan Markuš, Director of Silicon Alliances at Canonical. "Out-of-the-box RVA23 support for Ubuntu 26.04 LTS on SiFive’s BigSky datacenter development platform provides the enterprise software foundation required to accelerate AI and cloud-native workloads on RISC-V. We are proud to collaborate with SiFive, and this seamless integration ensures our mutual customers can confidently port enterprise workloads and accelerate their transition to RISC-V in the datacenter today.”

“Getting RHEL 10 running on SiFive’s BigSky platform isn't just a validation exercise, it’s proof that we’re moving from the 'theoretical' phase into production-grade reality. For our enterprise customers, the real win here isn't just performance metrics; it’s the architectural flexibility RISC-V offers without sacrificing the stability and software ecosystem they rely on.” said Brian “Redbeard” Harrington, Strategist, CTO Organization, Red Hat. “Through the RISE project, we’ve been working to ensure that when we say RISC-V is ready for the datacenter, we mean it. We’re excited to be partnering with SiFive to demonstrate that open standards can absolutely deliver the rigorous performance and reliability the modern datacenter demands."


**Key Platform Specifications**

The SiFive BigSky SF-2U870 is designed explicitly for advanced software porting, workload tuning, and validation testing to foster ecosystem enablement. Hardware specifications include:

  • 32 P870-D cores operating at 2.0 GHz
  • 256GB DDR5-5600 memory
  • 4x PCIe Gen5 x16 (64 lanes) and PCIe Gen3 x4
  • 2x 7.68TB U.2 NVMe SSD
  • 10/25Gb OCP 3.0 NIC

The platforms are available today and currently being produced in limited quantities to drive strategic development opportunities, with demand outpacing supply. Interested parties should contact SiFive sales for more information.

About SiFive

As the pioneers who founded RISC-V, SiFive is transforming the future of computing by bringing the power and flexibility of RISC-V to the world. SiFive achieved record growth in 2025 and its IP is featured in more than 500 designs, with over 10 billion cores shipped to date, SiFive is the trusted IP partner for the world’s most innovative technology companies.
